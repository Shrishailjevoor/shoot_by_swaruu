07 / Frequently asked

Before we
press record.

Answers to help you plan your shoot with confidence.

Does the request confirm my booking?

No. Your request is sent through WhatsApp. The studio must confirm availability, final pricing and agreed terms before the booking is confirmed.

Is travel included?

Travel, parking, venue fees and tolls are not automatically included. Share the location for a complete quote.

Can I request urgent delivery?

Yes, subject to availability. Confirm the deadline before the shoot; same-day delivery is not guaranteed.

Can I share a reference reel?

Yes. Add the reference link or describe the colours, pace, transitions, music and mood in your creative brief.

How will I receive the video?

Agree on the delivery method, format and timeline before production begins.
